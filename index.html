How it works

  1. 01

    Pick a board

    Play the ready-made board, or sign in with String ID to create a 3 × 3 or 5 × 5 board.

  2. 02

    Share the link

    Send your game link to the group. Players can open it on their phones.

  3. 03

    Complete a line

    Do the challenges and add photos to mark your squares. Fill a row, column or diagonal to get Bingo.
